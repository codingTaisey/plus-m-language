# 001 — 深く考えるためには、重く考えすぎないことが必要。

## Original

深く考えるためには、重く考えすぎないことが必要。

## English

To think deeply, you need to avoid taking things too seriously.

## Useful expressions

### take something too seriously

「〜を深刻に考えすぎる」

Example:

I tend to take things too seriously sometimes.

### think deeply

「深く考える」

Example:

I like to think deeply about things, but I don't want to overthink everything.

## Speaking

- Do you think it's possible to think deeply without overthinking?
- What's the difference between thinking deeply and taking something too seriously?
- Do you tend to take things too seriously?
